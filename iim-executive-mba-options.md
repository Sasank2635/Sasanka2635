# IIM Executive MBA Options: Conversation Notes

Date: 26 October 2026

## Question 1: Best IIMs for a senior software engineer

- Top IIMs by reputation and NIRF management ranking: IIM Ahmedabad, IIM Bangalore, IIM Calcutta, IIM Lucknow, IIM Kozhikode.
- IIM Udaipur offers Digital Enterprise Management, relevant for tech and digital product roles.
- Sources disagree on whether the NIRF 2026 management ranking is officially released. Treat rank order as provisional.
- Eligibility reminders: full-time programmes need a CAT score. Executive programmes need several years of work experience (3 to 5 years, depending on the programme).

## Question 2: Affordable IIMs with good NIRF rank and strong placements

- IIM Lucknow was described as having the best fee-to-salary ratio among the IIMs compared.
  - Placement report 2024-26: average ₹33.2 LPA, median ₹32.9 LPA.
- IIM Kozhikode: 2025 average about ₹28 LPA, median about ₹27 LPA (aggregator figures).

- IIT MBA programmes were noted as having lower tuition fees and strong placements, though they are not IIMs.

### IIM Ahmedabad PGPX (one-year executive MBA)

Verified against IIMA's official fee page (2026-27 batch):

- Programme fee: ₹35,00,000 (single accommodation, SSH) or ₹37,10,000 (married student housing, MSH).
- Covers tuition, books and course materials, accommodation, library, computing and network services.
- Not included: International Immersion Programme (IIP) cost, excluding travel and stay; utilities such as electricity, laundry and gym.
- The 2027-28 fee has not been published yet.

Admission and eligibility:

- Admission test: valid GMAT or GRE score (test-centre only; online or take-home scores are not accepted). Score must be within the last 5 years. One source says GMAT Focus is accepted.
- Not CAT-based.
- Minimum 4 years of full-time work experience after graduation.
- Minimum age 25 years; 15 years of total education.
- Admission is based on test scores, leadership profile, essays if shortlisted, and a faculty interview.

Correction: earlier in the conversation, figures of ₹10–12 lakh and ₹20 lakh were given for PGPX. Those were incorrect. The verified figure is ₹35 lakh.

## Key caveats

- Only the IIM Ahmedabad PGPX fee was confirmed from an IIMA official page (via a translated mirror). All other fees come from aggregator websites and often conflict.
- Fees, eligibility and admission tests change every year. Confirm each programme on the institute's official admissions page before applying.
- The user's years of work experience was not yet known. It determines which executive programmes are eligible: PGPX needs 4+ years, IIM Calcutta GMPE 5+ years, IIM Shillong 3+ years.
